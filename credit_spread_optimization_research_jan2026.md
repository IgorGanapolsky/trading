## 5. Rolling Techniques for Underwater Positions

### When to Roll vs. Close

**Three Options for Losing Trades:**
1. **CLOSE** - Take the loss if stop-loss hit or thesis invalidated
2. **ROLL** - If thesis intact and not at stop-loss
3. **HEDGE** - Add opposing position (complex, not recommended for $5K account)

**Your Current Strategy:** Roll "down and out" for credit if thesis intact ✓ **VALIDATED**

### Rolling Techniques - RESEARCH VALIDATED

**1. Rolling Out in Time (Most Common):**
- If < 14 DTE and underwater, roll to next month
- Collect additional premium to offset losses
- Wait for stock price to recover
- **CRITICAL:** Must receive NET CREDIT on the roll

**2. Rolling to New Delta Strikes:**
- Start at 20/13 deltas
- If spreads decay to 14/8 deltas, roll out to new 20/13 strikes
- Maintains consistent delta exposure
- Research shows this is OPTIMAL rolling approach

**3. Widening the Spread (CAUTION):**
- Increase spread width to collect more premium
- **WARNING:** This INCREASES maximum loss
- Only use if highly confident in thesis
- Not recommended for small accounts

**Your Current Approach:** "Roll down and out for credit" ✓ **CORRECT**

### Rolling Best Practices - NEW INSIGHTS

**When NOT to Roll:**
- Stop-loss is hit (take the loss)
- Thesis is invalidated (market conditions changed)
- Can't collect NET CREDIT on the roll
- Would require widening spread significantly

**Rolling at Credit vs. Debit:**
- Rolling at CREDIT = reduces risk, enhances returns ✓ **TARGET THIS**
- Rolling at DEBIT = increases risk, compounds losses ✗ **AVOID THIS**

**Delta-Based Rolling Rules:**
- If short strike reaches 35-40 delta, consider rolling or closing
- If spreads reach 50-60 delta, CLOSE or roll immediately
- Never let short strike go ITM (in-the-money) without action

**NEW RECOMMENDATION:**
Add to rolling rules:
- Only roll if can collect NET CREDIT
- Never roll if stop-loss is hit
- Monitor delta: if short strike > 40 delta, take action
- Maximum 2 rolls per position (after that, accept loss)
