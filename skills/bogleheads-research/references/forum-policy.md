# Bogleheads forum policy constraints

Authoritative page: <https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/rules>

Verified from the visible policy page on 2026-08-03. Recheck the page before any
public submission because forum rules can change.

## Drafting checklist

- Keep discussion civil, issue-focused, actionable, and in the correct forum.
- Use a meaningful topic title and explain any link rather than posting a bare
  URL.
- Avoid prohibited politics/religion, non-actionable arguments, market
  manipulation, solicitation, affiliate/referral links, and promotional posts.
- Company representatives must identify themselves and are limited to narrow
  factual or technical replies under the policy.
- Trim quotations to the minimum needed and cite the original source.
- Initial topics must contain the member's own question; AI may assist with
  formatting.
- Replies must not primarily consist of AI-generated material. Any retained
  AI-generated content must be labeled and include its sources and the query
  used to generate it.
- Never ingest or expose private messages, account data, email addresses,
  cookies, credentials, or other non-public member information.

## Submission boundary

Perusing, indexing, searching, and drafting are read-only/local actions. Posting
a topic or reply, sending a private message, voting, following a topic, or
changing a profile is a separate public/account side effect. Require the exact
destination and reviewed content at action time, then verify the resulting post
on the forum.
