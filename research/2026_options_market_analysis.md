## 9. Timing Recommendations

### When to Enter Credit Spreads

#### BEST TIMES TO ENTER (Q1 2026)

**Optimal Weekly Windows:**
- **Monday-Tuesday:** Lowest implied volatility, best prices
- **Thursday-Friday:** Avoid - weekend risk premium inflates prices

**Optimal Monthly Windows:**
- **Now through Jan 31:** Ride Q1 rally momentum, favorable seasonality
- **Early March (1st-10th):** Position for spring strength
- **AVOID late Feb:** Potential profit-taking, increased volatility

**Intraday Timing:**
- **Best:** 10:00am-11:00am ET (post-open stabilization)
- **Acceptable:** 2:00pm-3:00pm ET (afternoon calm)
- **AVOID:** First 30 min (9:30-10:00am) and last hour (3:00-4:00pm) - widest spreads

#### MARKET CONDITIONS FOR ENTRY

Enter when ALL conditions met:
1. ✅ VIX <18 (low volatility regime)
2. ✅ Ticker trending neutral-to-bullish (5-day, 20-day MA alignment)
3. ✅ No major earnings/economic reports within 7 days
4. ✅ Implied volatility NOT spiking (check IV rank <50%)
5. ✅ Market hours only (9:30am-4:00pm ET)

### When to AVOID Entering

❌ **Never Enter When:**
- VIX >20 (volatility too high, spreads too wide)
- Major economic reports next 48 hours (CPI, Fed decision, NFP)
- Friday afternoon (weekend risk)
- Ticker down >2% that day (wait for stabilization)
- Bid-ask spread >10% of credit (liquidity too poor)

### When to Exit Credit Spreads

#### PROFIT TAKING (Priority Order)
1. **50% Max Profit** - PRIMARY exit target
   - Why: Captures most theta decay, frees capital, improves win rate
   - Example: Collected $0.70 credit, buy back at $0.35
2. **75% Max Profit** - Alternative for strong conviction trades
   - Why: Capture more gain if trend very strong
   - Risk: Diminishing returns, tying up capital
3. **7 DTE Time Stop** - Close regardless of P&L
   - Why: Gamma risk accelerates, assignment risk increases

#### LOSS MANAGEMENT (MANDATORY)
1. **2x Credit Received** - HARD STOP LOSS
   - Example: Sold for $0.70, close at $1.40 loss
   - Why: Prevents catastrophic losses, per CLAUDE.md rules
2. **Thesis Invalidation** - Exit immediately
   - Example: Fed unexpectedly hikes rates, major geopolitical event
3. **VIX Spike >25** - Consider closing all positions
   - Why: Regime change, volatility expansion threatens all spreads

#### ROLLING DECISION TREE
```
Is spread threatened (short strike tested)?
├─ YES → Is thesis still intact?
│   ├─ YES → Is stop-loss NOT hit?
│   │   ├─ YES → ROLL down and out for net credit
│   │   └─ NO → CLOSE position, accept loss
│   └─ NO → CLOSE position, move on
└─ NO → Hold to profit target
```
