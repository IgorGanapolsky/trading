# Freedom Builder process steal (high-ROI only)

Source: riconasol.com/bootcamp — income portfolio bootcamp marketing.

## What we stole

- Freedom Number — define monthly after-tax income that makes work optional (maps to North Star 6000)
- 3-Bucket System — every dollar has a job: Ops/Safety, Lab (paper validation), Field/Passive (gated)
- 30-day paced build — two modules per week unlock; worksheets over binge watching
- Messy action — first paper structure beats perfect plan never funded
- Not stock picks / not signals — explicit honesty flags

## What we did NOT steal

- Dividend ticker lists or what to buy
- Course content, branding, pricing, refund policy
- Claim that any yield is guaranteed

## Commands

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/freedom_capital_plan.py --monthly 6000 --liquid 100000 --day 1
.venv/bin/python scripts/freedom_capital_plan.py --from-system-state --monthly 6000 --liquid 130000
.venv/bin/python scripts/freedom_capital_plan.py --number-only --monthly 6000
```

## Relation to put-credit lab

Lab bucket funds paper validation. Field live capital stays blocked until EDGE_CANDIDATE.
Passive capital math uses an assumed yield for planning only — not put-credit expectancy.
